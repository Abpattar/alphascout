========================================================================
ALPHASCOUT — SIMPLIFIED PROBLEMS & PREFERRED SOLUTIONS
========================================================================
Plain-language summary of every issue found so far, and the fix
preferred for each. Ordered by priority — fix top to bottom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 1: Your stock list and your filter reject each other
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  You picked ~150 famous sector stocks (HAL, BEL, IRCTC, SUZLON etc.)
  by hand. But your filter only allows small stocks (price 50-500).
  Most famous names have grown too big, so only 14 stocks survive.
  You built a list that was never going to pass your own filter.

Preferred solution:
  Stop hand-picking stocks by sector first. Instead:
    1. Pull the FULL list of small-cap stocks from Screener.in using
       just the price/size/volume filters (no sector bias).
    2. AFTER that list exists, tag each stock with a sector label
       (defence, EV, rail, etc.) if it matches your keywords.
  Sector becomes a label added after, not a gate applied before.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 2: The bot can only confirm stocks it already knows, never
discover new ones from news
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  Right now, an article is only read further if it mentions a stock
  ALREADY in your 14-stock list. So if good news breaks about a small
  stock not yet tracked, it gets thrown away before the AI even sees
  it. This means your "AI finds opportunities from news" idea doesn't
  actually work yet — it can only re-confirm stocks you already added.

Preferred solution:
  Let the ticker-matching step check article text against the FULL
  NSE stock list (or a name/alias list), not just your small 14-stock
  universe. Only AFTER a stock is found in an article should you check
  if it passes your safety filters (price, market cap, liquidity).
  Filtering should happen after discovery, not before.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 3: New stocks found on-the-fly might skip your safety checks
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  When the bot finds a brand-new ticker mid-analysis, it validates it
  using a quick yfinance lookup. But it's unclear if this quick check
  applies the SAME safety rules (minimum liquidity, no SEBI
  surveillance stocks, minimum listing age) as your main weekly
  universe build. If not, risky/illiquid stocks could sneak in through
  this shortcut.

Preferred solution:
  Make on-the-fly ticker validation call the exact same filter
  function used in the weekly universe build. One filter function,
  used everywhere — no separate "lighter" version for new discoveries.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 4: News-reaction is naturally slow
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  By the time news is scraped and read, the stock has often already
  moved. Running only twice a day (6:30 AM, 4:30 PM) makes this worse.

Preferred solution:
  Treat price/volume spikes as the main trigger (checked more often,
  every 15-30 min), and use news only to CONFIRM whether the move has
  a real reason behind it. Screener-first mode should be the main
  path; news scraping becomes the supporting evidence, not the
  starting point.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 5: LLM "confidence" score isn't proven accurate
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  When the AI says "90% confident," that doesn't mean it's actually
  right 90% of the time. Auto-trading based on this number is risky
  until you've checked whether it's true.

Preferred solution:
  Log every signal for months without auto-trading. Compare stated
  confidence vs what actually happened. Use your own real win-rate
  data to correct the AI's confidence score before ever auto-executing
  trades. Require a much larger sample (30-50+ signals) per confidence
  bucket before trusting it — 5 signals is not enough to be sure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 6: No proof yet that the strategy works
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  The backtesting tool now exists, but has it actually been run on
  real past data yet? Building the tool isn't the same as knowing the
  results.

Preferred solution:
  Actually run the backtest on 1-2 years of real historical news +
  prices. Look at the real win rate, profit factor, and max drawdown
  before trusting the system with money — even paper money.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 7: Company press releases can be disguised pump news
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  A company's own press release praising itself isn't independent
  news — it could be designed to pump the stock before insiders sell.

Preferred solution (already partly built):
  Keep source tiering (govt > mainstream news > niche sites >
  company PR). Keep the PR/pump detection prompt. Make sure PR-only
  news, with no independent confirmation, never triggers a signal by
  itself.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 8: Small stocks can be hard to exit
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  A small stock can hit a "lower circuit" (price freeze) and become
  impossible to sell, turning a small loss into a stuck position.

Preferred solution (already partly built):
  Keep minimum daily traded value filters, position size limits, and
  the weekly drawdown kill-switch. Also check a stock's recent circuit
  history before entering — recent circuit hits should count against
  a stock, not in its favor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 9: This project could cross into regulated territory
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  In India, generating and sharing buy/sell signals with others
  (even friends) may require SEBI registration as a Research Analyst
  or Investment Adviser. This is a real legal rule, not just a
  guideline.

Preferred solution:
  Keep this strictly personal-use only for now (your own Telegram,
  your own trading account). Don't share signals with others or
  monetize until SEBI's rules have actually been read and understood.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 10: LLM usage might run out of free budget mid-run
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  Each article goes through 5 separate AI calls. Running this twice a
  day across many articles could use up your free daily AI quota
  before the run even finishes, causing silent quality drops.

Preferred solution:
  Track how many tokens/calls are actually used per full run. Confirm
  it comfortably fits inside the free tier limits, or add caps so the
  system stops gracefully instead of silently degrading mid-run.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM 11: Confidence calibration needs live data, not memory
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simple version:
  The calibration system compares stated confidence to real results —
  but only works if someone checks stock prices a few days after
  every signal and records what happened. If this step isn't
  automatic, the calibration data goes stale and becomes useless.

Preferred solution:
  Set up a scheduled job that automatically checks each open signal's
  result a few days later and records it — don't rely on manually
  remembering to do this.
